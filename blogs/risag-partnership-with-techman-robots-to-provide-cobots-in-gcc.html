Risag Partnership with Techman Robots to provide Cobots in GCC

  • event_available 20-08-2026

The manufacturing sectors of the GCC are undergoing rapid transformation, pacing along with Industry 4.0. With smart factories and IoT (Internet of Things) taking the front stage, businesses are looking for automation solutions that are flexible, cost-effective, and easy to integrate into the production line.

Take robots, for instance. They worked in isolation, caged and separated from human operators; now they meet their new and advanced successors, cobots/ collaborative robots. Designed to work alongside human operators, cobots make the long-held dream of humans come true—a robot-human collaboration that actually works side by side.

To make cobot automation more accessible across the industries in the GCC, Risag Robotics has partnered with Techman Robots, the renowned cobot manufacturer based in Taiwan. Together, we aim to deliver collaborative robot solutions to support manufacturers in building a smarter and more advanced workspace.

About Techman Robot

Techman Robots is a Taiwan-based robotics company that specializes in AI-powered cobots designed to make automation more smart and simple. Found in 2015, Techman is renowned for the manufacturing of cobots with advanced features such as built-in machine vision, enabling the cobots to see, inspect, and perform tasks without needing any separate vision system. Their cobot lineup stands out for their unique integration of robotics, AI vision, and intuitive software into a single platform.

Why This Partnership Matters of GCC Industries

Addressing workforce challenges while maintaining the speed and quality of production weighs upon the industries of GCC more than you think. With the government leading efforts to digitize the industries and actively promoting smart digitization strategies, a flexible automation solution has become a necessity.

This is where the partnership between Risag and Techman Robot becomes relevant. We help businesses in the region to get easier access to advanced cobot technology, along with the support of a local team that understands the requirements of the regional industries.

As mentioned above, our collaboration helps manufacturers across the GCC to deal with labor shortages, meet the production demands, and have faster delivery expectations, all while keeping the costs under control.

Techman Cobot Solutions Offered By Risag Robotics

Through this partnership, Risag Robotics will offer a versatile range of cobots designed for different industrial applications, such as the following:

  • Machine tending
  • Pick and place
  • Quality inspection
  • Assembly operations
  • Package and palletizing

Our flexible solutions can be integrated into both the new and existing production lines, making automation practical and easier.

Risag Robotics’ Role Beyond Distribution

Yes, purchase isn’t the end line of our commitment because every business has unique requirements, challenges, and production goals. We work closely with our customers throughout their automation journey.

From consultation, solution design, installation, programming, operator training, commissioning, and prompt after-sales support, we work hand in hand with our clients to make sure that their purchase is worthwhile.

Partnering with Techman Robots, we successfully combine advanced technology with local support and expertise, helping businesses to choose, install, and maintain the right cobot solution.

Looking Ahead

Government and industries are looking forward to and heavily investing in smart manufacturing and advanced technologies to meet the growing customer expectations and evolving market needs. As the production scale rises, strong and reliable support is necessary for the workers to maintain operational efficiency.

With a smart and incredibly useful asset like collaborative robots on hand, businesses can skyrocket their production lines, asserting their expertise in the competitive landscape. And as responsible and reliable automation partners, Risag Robotics’ aim is to make automation easier and accessible to business, helping them adopt with advanced robotics solution with confidence.
