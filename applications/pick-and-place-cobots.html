Why Automate Pick and Place Operations?

01

No more repetitive strain

When employees are required to do picking, lifting, and placing repeatedly, they might develop fatigue and strain over time. Cobots are designed to perform repetitive tasks. This lets workers shift to more important work.

02

Consistent cycle times

Once a pick-and-place program is set, it is infinitely repeatable with a pick-and-place cobot system. This maintains a predictable workflow and eliminates any chance for variations compared to doing this manually.

03

Reduced labor in production

When the repetitive handling process is automated, workers can focus on production tasks that require skilled supervision, such as inspection and machine inspection, to name a few.

How to Choose the Right Pick and Place Cobot

Consider these factors before getting an automation system for your requirement

01

Payload—How Heavy Is the Part?

Check and make sure of the product weight with a gripper and attached tools.

02

Reach—How Far Must It Move?

Measure the required reach—the distance between pickup and placement points should be easily accessible for the robot.

03

Speed—How Many Cycles Do You Need?

Determine the required number of cycles based on how many products you need to be moved per minute.

04

Gripper—How Will It Hold the Product?

Choose a gripper based on the product’s shape, weight, surface, and material.

05

Vision—Does the Product Stay in the Same Position?

The vision system helps keep track of the products’ position and identify any variations before picking them.
