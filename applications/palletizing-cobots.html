Palletizing

Why Automate Palletizing?

Manual palletizing takes time and subjects workers to fatigue. With palletizing automation, this repetitive task is carried out by cobots, letting workers handle other important activities.

01

Reduce Heavy Manual Lifting

Repeatedly lifting and stacking cartons can cause physical strain and fatigue. A cobot palletizer handles the repetitive movement and reduces the physical workload on your team.

02

Maintain Consistent Stacking

Manual stacking flow can vary between shifts. One group follows one method, and the other one on the next shift may abide by another. Cobots palletize in unison. Same arrangement throughout completion with steady consistency and efficiency.

03

Free Up Your Workforce

You can leave the repetitive pallet building to robots. The freed-up time can be effectively utilized for packing, quality checks, production monitoring, and other important tasks.

Palletizing Cobot or Traditional Palletizing Robot: Which Is Right for You?

You get effective automation with both, but for different requirements. If you require flexible operation across changing jobs, choose a cobot. But if you are handling high-volume routine tasks, cobots offer better efficiency.

The process

How Does a Cobot Palletizing System Work?

Firstly, a pallet pattern is set. It includes product dimensions, number of boxes, stacking arrangement, and final pallet weight. Secondly, a suitable gripper is chosen for the product. This enables the cobot to pick the product up from the conveyor. After picking up the product, the cobot places it in the programmed position. Finally, after thoroughly testing the settings, gripper choice, and stacking pattern, the necessary safety arrangements and operator training precede regular operation.

Choosing a cobot

How to Choose the Right Palletizing Cobot

Choosing a cobot goes beyond just product weight. The robot should be able to move seamlessly between pickup and placement positions.

01

Payload—How Heavy Is Your Product?

Calculate the product weight, and add in gripper, mounting plate, and other tooling weights.

02

Reach—How High and Wide Must It Stack?

Here, consider the pallet dimensions, final height, and distance between the pickup point and the pallet.

03

Cycle Time—How Many Boxes Must It Stack?

Cycle time determines how many boxes are stacked per minute. This alone can determine whether the chosen cobot is apt for the work or not.

04

Product Type – What Is Being Handled?

Everything about the item being picked and placed, including box dimensions, surface material, weight distribution, and packaging strength, determines the type of gripper that will be used.

05

Production Layout—Where Will the Cobot Work?

For better robot placement and safety arrangements, thoroughly understand the available floor space, conveyor positions, and pallet location, along with worker movement.
