Why Automate Welding with a Cobot?

Welding automation does not cut off jobs. Instead, it takes over repetitive welding tasks, preserving human expertise for more complex work.

1. Work Through Skilled Welder Shortages

Cobots handle recurring welding paths more efficiently. This bridges the gap between production requirements and hiring more welders. Experienced workers can shift towards skill-oriented welding operations while cobots handle repetitive tasks with high precision.

2. More Consistent Welds, Less Rework

Cobots offer unmatched consistency once their paths are programmed. This boosts productivity and adds enhanced output to repeatable welding tasks.

3. Reduce Repetitive Exposure

Automation doesn’t cover fume extraction or protective measures. They still have to be done even when cobots take over repetitive welding, letting workers shift towards more skill-oriented tasks.

What Welding Jobs Can a Cobot Handle?

Cobotic operations deliver the best results when tasks are repetitive for the most part, such as movements, part positioning, or welding paths.

  • Repeat-Part Seam Welding for effective repetitive joint welding
  • Long-Seam welding for welding long joints on a preset path
  • Small-Batch production jobs can be automated
  • Providing fixed positioning for parts

Welding Cobot or Traditional Welding Robot: Which Should You Choose?

Both fall under welding automation but fulfill different objectives.

What to Compare Welding Cobot Traditional Welding Robot
Programming Easier to set up and teach new welding movements. Usually needs a trained robot programmer.
Space Required Can fit into smaller workshops. Usually needs more space for the robot and safety barriers.
Flexibility Can be moved or adjusted for different jobs. Usually set up for one specific production task.
Production Needs Suitable for repeat jobs and smaller production batches. Better suited for large-scale, continuous production.
Cost Can be a more affordable way to start using welding automation. Can cost more, depending on the system size and setup.

How Does a Welding Cobot System Work?

Cobot-led welding processes are best executed with a complete setup, including the torch, power supply, components, fixtures, programming, and safety tools.

A simple breakdown:

  • Understanding the material, thickness, length, volume, and cycle time
  • Choosing a cobot that suits the above measures along with a torch and fixtures
  • Choosing start and end points, torch movement, travel path, and welding parameters
  • A test is carried out, and the equipment is prepared for production
